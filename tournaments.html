CALL OF DUTY

Respawn Cup

A team competition focused on objective play, communication and consistency.

Format: Single elimination   /   Series: Best of three Hardpoint maps

View tournament rules
  1. Each team names four starting players.
  2. Teams agree the map order before the series.
  3. The first team to win two maps advances.
  4. Both captains confirm the result after the series.
  5. Players must treat opponents respectfully and follow the agreed game settings.
02
5 v 5
VALORANT

Tactical Cup

A competition for teams that enjoy careful planning and coordinated rounds.

Format: Single elimination   /   Series: Best of one map

View tournament rules
  1. Each team names five starting players.
  2. Captains agree on a map and custom match settings.
  3. The team that wins the map advances.
  4. Any disputed result is reviewed before the next match.

2 v 2
ROCKET LEAGUE

Duos Cup

Find a partner and practise the passing, positioning and teamwork needed to succeed.

Format: Single elimination   /   Series: Best of five games

View tournament rules
  1. Each team has two players.
  2. Games use the standard five-minute timer.
  3. A tied game continues into overtime.
  4. The first team to win three games advances.
