Discover competitive games and the teamwork behind them with Rival.

Our purpose

This website helps players explore games, understand competition formats and learn about team roles. It is aimed at people who are curious about organised competitive gaming.

Rival is not affiliated with the publishers of the games mentioned.

Community guide

  • Respect teammates, opponents and organisers.
  • Play fairly and follow the agreed rules.
  • Keep personal information private.
  • Take breaks and keep gaming balanced with other commitments.

Can I enter a tournament?

Registration is not available. The events shown are examples of competition formats.

Does the website work offline?

Yes. All six pages, the stylesheet and all images are included. Only external websites require internet access.

Are my details collected?

This downloadable website has no accounts, tracking tools, cookies or forms that collect personal information.
