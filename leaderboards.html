How the points work

Teams receive three points for a match win and zero points for a loss. Matches have no draws.

  1. Teams are ranked by total points.
  2. If points are equal, compare the teams’ head-to-head results.
  3. If they are still tied, use a deciding match.
Is this the same as the tournament schedule?

No. This table represents a separate practice league. The cups on the tournaments page use elimination brackets.
