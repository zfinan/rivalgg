How the example prices work

Entry is shown per player. The total pool combines both sides' entries. For example, four players entering a 2v2 at £5 each create a £20 pool for the winning team, not £20 per player.

These are suggested custom formats, not official publisher competition rules. Playlist and custom-lobby availability must be checked before arranging a real match.
